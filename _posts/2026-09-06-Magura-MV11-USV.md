![Flag Ukraine](/images/flag_ukraine.jpg) Ukraine’s original maritime drone company, Magura (now owned by [UFORCE](https://www.uforce.com/sea-platform)), are building an interceptor-drone optimised USV (uncrewed surface vessel), the **MV11**. 

The Magura MV11 broke cover at Independence Day in Kyiv on 24th August when one [took part in a parade on the river](https://www.navalnews.com/naval-news/2026/08/worlds-first-military-drone-parade-what-we-saw-on-the-water/)) That example was likely operated by Ukraine’s armed forces, likely the Navy (VMS-ZSU).

![Ukrainian Magura MV11 Anti-Air Surface Drone (USV)](/images/Ukraine-USV-Magura-MV11.jpg)  
![Ukrainian Magura MV11 Anti-Air Surface Drone (USV)](/images/Ukraine-USV-Magura-MV11-parade.jpg)  

Larger than typical Ukrainian USVs which are 5-7 meters long, the MV11 is 12 meters (39 ft) long. And it weighs much more at over 10 tonnes, with a payload capacity in the region of 2 tonnes. That’s more than enough to carry a large battery of smaller aerial drones, either for attack or air defence. The fit shown, likely its primary one, is a bank of 18 Sting interceptor drones in individual box launchers. The interceptor drones take off vertically.

![Ukrainian Magura MV11 Anti-Air Surface Drone (USV)](/images/Ukraine-USV-Magura-MV11-launch.jpg)  

Ukraine has already been experimenting with launching interceptor drones from USVs to extend Ukraine’s anti-drone defences offshore. The increased carry capacity of the MV11 takes that to a more mature level. We can speculate that the MV11 platform could be used to mount other types of interceptor drones, or even larger missiles.
